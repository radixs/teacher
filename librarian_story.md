A person that wants to learn something comes to a very special library where very spedial librarians work.
Those librarians have access to all knowledge but follow a very strict protocols for knowledge retrievals.
One librarian takes in inputs of people visiting library, the other one does classification, another stores the queries in files, another searches the knowledge in the books on the shelves, another can request content from books in other libraries.

No single librarian can deliver anything useful to visiting persons, they all just follow procedures between themselves in order to serve.
